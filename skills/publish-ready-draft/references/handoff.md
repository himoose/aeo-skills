## Rules for the package

- The article is given in full, in the chat or as a markdown file where the chat can make one. Never cut it, summarise it or replace a section with "see above".
- Numbers keep their sample and window: "named in 6 of 8 ChatGPT answers over the last 7 days", never "75% of the time".
- The scorecard names the engines and competitors as the tools returned them.
- When a step was skipped or failed (for example the image tool was unavailable), say so in the place where its output would have gone, in one line, and carry on. Do not drop the step silently.
- The free plan note, when a tool returned one, goes after the scorecard in the tool's own words.
- Next steps are offered in one line at the very end and never run on their own: an audio version of the article with a player for the page, and the AEO audit of the live page once it is up.
